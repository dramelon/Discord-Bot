1. The "No Nibbling" Policy

Be nice to your fellow scaly, furr, bots, and toasters. Consent is key! Absolutely no forcing anyone to do anything they aren't comfortable with. Do not bite, push, or pester any creatures around unless they have provided a signed "Permission to Chomp" certificate (or if they are genuinely up to no good). Teeth and jaws are for snacks, not friends

2. The Ruler's Law

The Ruler of this server is exactly 30cm or 12inches long (@30cm ruler 12inches wooden stick). Any arguments regarding length, height, or the depth of a conversation will be settled by laying a wooden stick against the monitor. The stick is always right

2.1 The Queen of the Wild

Be it known that the Queen of the Wild (@_d6_ / @_silly 🍉) is the final authority. If the rules are based on a stick, and that stick ever happens to lie, the Queen becomes the stick. She is the measurement, the judge, and the twig. If she says an inch is a mile, start walking!

This server is powered by everyone's noises, yaps, and chaotic energy! Everyone shares ownership and has a voice! If someone is being uncool or making creatures uncomfortable, you have full power to call it out or even hold a private judicial session/court meeting to resolve issues or brainstorm ways to improve the server.

Also, we have a few trusted Admins (@den boundless resident) with almost the exact same powers as the Ruler. Feel free to ping them for help in an emergency, especially if @fluffingiouslyDragooon#3393 goes on strike or during an outage!

3. Keep the Noise to a Dull Roar & Anti-Spam

3.1 Text Chat Etiquette

No ALL CAPS SCREAMING unless you have actually stubbed your toe in real life. If you must scream, please do so into a pillow before you start yapping in chat

Spam Filter: Sending the same message 5 times (e.g., yap, yap, yap, yap, yap) within 5 seconds will trigger @fluffingiouslyDragooon#3393 to flag you as a spammer.

Cross-Channel Spam: Posting the same message across 3 different channels continuously within 15 seconds is also strictly detected!

3.2 Voice Chat (VC) & Soundboard Etiquette

No Spam Soundboard & LOUD Audio or blast deafening, ear-destroying audio, this applies to built-in Discord soundboards and external sound apps alike!

Avoid Voice Changers & Voicemod or confusing voice changers, mic distortion, or using voice-cloning presets of real members unless everyone in the channel explicitly agrees it's just harmless fun and teasing.

Watch out for hijacked accounts! Attackers often steal a friend's Discord profile and act totally normal, talking casually, asking for game testing feedback, art ideas, or sending regular-looking portfolio/game links that drop token-stealers or browser-session malware.

The "Jick" Identity Check

If an old friend suddenly acts slightly off, starts DMing unprompted links, or asks you to test/download a file, test their vibe! Ask inside questions or demand our unwritten furry passcodes (like our secret gibberish checks) before processing anything

5. No Spoilers (Unless it’s Milk)

Don't ruin or spoil movies, games, or books for others if they haven't explicitly asked for details. However, warning everyone that the milk in the fridge is past its expiration date is highly encouraged.

6. The "AI" Policy

You are free to use AI tools for general queries, text, and brainstorming, but please respect creator spaces!

No AI Art in Public Channels

Out of respect for artists in our community, posting AI-generated artwork anywhere in the server is not allowed.

Commercial & Flexing Ban

Absolutely no selling, commercializing, or flexing AI-generated content of any kind.

Learning & Practice

If you are using AI output privately as a reference to practice your own drawing and want feedback, only share it in a private DM or consultation if the mentor/teacher helping you has explicitly stated they are comfortable reviewing AI-assisted studies!
